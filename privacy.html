MuverAI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 September 2026

Who we are

MuverAI ("we", "us") provides an AI music-production mobile app. The entity responsible for your information under this policy is MuverAI.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: an anonymous device-scoped identifier by default; an email address only if you choose to create an account.
  • Content you provide: song prompts, lyrics, style/vocal selections, and the audio tracks generated for you.
  • Usage & device: app version, OS/version, coarse language/region, IP address (transient, for request routing/security), and basic event logs.
  • Discover: if you publish a song, its title, generated artwork, genre, lyrics (for vocal songs), your chosen artist name, and play/like counts become public.

2. How we use it

To generate the music you request; to operate accounts, credits and subscriptions; to keep the service secure and prevent abuse; and to comply with law.

3. AI processing & third parties (sub-processors)

ProviderPurposeData sent
ElevenLabs Music/audio generation Your text prompt, lyrics, style parameters
Supabase Authentication, database, file storage, backend functions Account identifier, your projects, generated audio
Apple / Google App distribution and in-app purchases Purchase/transaction identifiers

These providers process your data only to perform their service and are contractually barred from using it to train their own models or for their own purposes.

4. Storage & retention

Generated audio and project data are stored on Supabase infrastructure and retained while your account is active. Deleting a song removes it. Deleting your account removes your personal data except where retention is legally required.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, export, objection, and withdrawal of consent — contact muverai.support@gmail.com.

Regional notes: EEA/UK users have GDPR rights and may complain to a supervisory authority. California users have CCPA rights, and we do not sell personal information.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the local minimum age); we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (TLS); access is restricted; no method is perfectly secure.

8. Changes

We post updates here with a new effective date.
