Data Deletion

How to delete your MuverAI account and data · Hesap ve veri silme

Delete in the app

  1. Open MuverAI and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap Hesabımı sil (Delete my account) and confirm.

Your account, your songs and the data linked to them are deleted.

Delete by email

If you can no longer open the app, email muverai.support@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask for your data to be deleted. We handle requests within 30 days.
